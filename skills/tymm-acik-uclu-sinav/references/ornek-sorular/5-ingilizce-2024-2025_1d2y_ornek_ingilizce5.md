daily routines.

Yağız Kağan Erdoğmuş is a young chess player from Türkiye. He has the title 
of Grandmaster. Yağız wakes up early every morning. He has breakfast with 
his family. After breakfast, he goes to school. He plays chess after school every 
day. Then, he studies and reads books. He sleeps early every night because he 
believes good sleep is important for success in chess.
a.	 Who is Yağız Kaan Erdoğmuş?
b.	 Does Yağız get up early every morning?
c.	 What does Yağız do after breakfast?
d.	 When does Yağız play chess?
e.	 Why does Yağız go to bed early at night?
